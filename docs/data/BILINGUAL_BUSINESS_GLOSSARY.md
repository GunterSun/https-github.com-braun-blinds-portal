## Governance
- Do not use “Shutters” and “Blinds” as the same Chinese term. Shutters = 百叶窗; Blinds = 百叶帘.
- `0/16` is not displayed. Whole-inch values display as `72"`.
- Translation changes must be reviewed centrally and versioned.
- User-entered names, addresses, notes, SKUs, order numbers, tracking numbers, monetary amounts, and measurements are not automatically rewritten.
