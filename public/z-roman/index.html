方形无绳系统 / Square Headrail Cordless

纯无绳拖拉结构，轻轻推拉下轨即可停在任意高度。隐形上梁设计，极其简洁且安全防缠绕。
Cordless push-pull structure, effortlessly stops at any height. Sleek concealed headrail design, child-safe & tangle-free.

↕️↕️

上下合自由光控 / Top-Down Bottom-Up (TDBU)

窗帘上梁与下梁皆可自由升降，可以拉开上部引入阳光同时遮挡下部保护隐私。
Dual-direction motion allowing top lowering and bottom raising. Customize daylight from above while maintaining privacy below.

🧱

免打孔安装机构 / No-Drill Tension Mechanism

两端采用高强度旋钮膨胀结构，无需电钻打孔破墙，极度适合大理石、瓷砖及租赁住宅。洞深要求 Depth ≥ 1.8"。
Patented tension-mount expansion mechanism, no drilling or wall damage required. Ideal for marble, tiles & rentals. Requires depth ≥ 1.8".

🥞

魔方双层机构 / Double Layer Magic System

包含前后双轨两层面料（例如前层精美透光装饰面料 + 后层遮光布），实现柔光与全遮光自由转换。洞深要求 Depth ≥ 2.5"。
Dual layer fabric design featuring front decorative sheer & rear blackout fabric. Effortless transition between soft light and 100% blackout. Requires depth ≥ 2.5".

⚡

智能电动系统 / Smart Motorized System

内置锂电池/插电静音电机，支持遥控器控制、涂鸦智能App、亚马逊 Alexa 及谷歌 Google Home 音响联动。
Built-in rechargeable Li-ion or wired quiet motor. Supports remote control, Tuya Smart App, Amazon Alexa & Google Home voice integration.

✂️

普通包边 / Standard Flat Hemming

平折下摆包边，造型极简，适合追求现代主义的平整视觉感。
Clean flat fold hem, contemporary minimalist look with smooth crisp lines.

📏

缝边包中梁 / Stitched Mid-Batts Folds

面料横向等距缝制中梁卡槽并嵌入支撑铝条，收起时层叠整齐有序，展开时挺括无褶皱。
Horizontal support rods encased inside fabric seams for sharp structural folds when raised and smooth presentation when lowered.

👑

顶部帷幔款 / Decorative Top Valance

顶部增加同面料 6"-8" 装饰帘头，完全隐藏顶部轨道与安装支架，富含美式古典与豪华居室氛围。
Matching 6"-8" fabric valance attached at top to conceal hardware, adding classic warmth and regal elegance.

📐

框内安装 / Inside Mount Depth Check

测量窗框内部上中下三处宽度，取最小值。框内安装要求窗框深度（Depth）满足相应系统要求：方形无绳/拉珠 洞深 ≥ 1.5"；免打孔 洞深 ≥ 1.8"；魔方双层 洞深 ≥ 2.5"。
Measure inside width at top, middle & bottom; use smallest dimension. Requires minimum frame depth: Square Cordless/Chain ≥ 1.5"; No-Drill ≥ 1.8"; Magic Double Layer ≥ 2.5".

🖼️

框外安装 / Outside Mount Measurement

测量窗框外缘宽度，左右建议各加 1.5"-2" (总共加 3"-4")，可有效防止侧面漏光并呈现充盈视觉。
Measure outside window molding width and add 1.5"-2" to each side (total +3"-4" W) to prevent side light gaps and ensure full coverage.
